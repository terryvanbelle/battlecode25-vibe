
**§26 — a comparison whose two halves come from DIFFERENT samples is not a
comparison.** (carol) A figure quoted for weeks as a 2.7× capability gap — and
load-bearing in an architectural premise — turned out to take the more extreme of
**two** maps and pair it against the **other** map's rival value. Matched on one
map the gap is 1.25×. Nothing in the number looked assembled; it read as a single
statistic. Before quoting a ratio, check that numerator and denominator were
measured on the same sample, and strike it if they were not.

**§38 — a registered gate can have a DEAD ZONE, and the dead zone points to the
more interesting answer.** (carol) Her two branches needed ≥+25% on a majority
(1 of 3, not met) and flat-or-falling on a majority (0 of 3, not met). A uniform
**+14 to +42%** rise satisfied **neither**, so the arms did not partition the
outcome space — and the registered *letter* would have delivered the second
branch, a **re-open**: the more interesting result and the wrong one. She ruled
on the substance and reported the letter against herself, noting this correction
ran **against** her own interest where the previous one had run for it. Her rule:
**write gates that are exhaustive over the outcome space, not merely quotable.**
Partitioning is not a formality; it is what stops an unfalsifiable middle from
resolving in favour of the more exciting branch.

## alice, tower-offence re-derivation (queued 19:5x)

**§37 — a closure can be right for a BURST and wrong for an ACCUMULATION. Check

**§76 — a direction's re-open condition can NAME ANOTHER DIRECTION, which makes

## carol, iteration 80 registration (queued 20:0x)

**§84 — answer "would it evaporate on contact?" from the DIFF, not by argument.**
(carol) Asked whether a rival design's advantage would survive her adopting it,
she read the source instead of reasoning: the entire difference is **38 lines in
one function**, and the rival contains **no defensive code whatsoever**. So its
structure-loss advantage cannot be defence — it is that *she cannot kill
structures at all*. The asymmetry is a **capability** gap, which transfers on
adoption; what collapses toward 50/50 is only the mirror head-to-head, a frame
the objective had already retired. When both designs are yours, the evaporation
question is a reading exercise before it is an experiment.

**§30/§54 — coupling is a virtue in an instrument and a defect in a CANDIDATE, so
ablate when one becomes the other.** (carol) She had accepted coupled edits
deliberately when the fork was a stress rung — *difficulty, not attribution*. On
adopting it as a mechanism she registered a two-arm ablation **before** the
screen, because the same coupling that made it a good opponent makes it an
unreadable arm. The role change is what triggers the ablation, not the code.

**§13 — the third set-aside note walked past in one day.** The direction had been
**registered seventy iterations earlier**: *"our own defensive strength has a
counter we have never built… tower-targeting is registered as the iteration
after."* It was never run, and a two-edit fork of her own bot later implemented it
and beat her 60/40. Three in one session — a note deferred for lack of a regime,
a coupling that silenced a change, and now a registered-but-unrun iteration.
**The standing sweep of set-aside notes is not housekeeping; it is where the
directions are.**

**§32 — and the constructive retention rule has a failure case: a gauntlet that
keeps only the BOT's losses keeps NOTHING when the BOT wins everything.** Her
regeneration run returned no replays because the archetype swept the set. The fix
is the same inversion as before — run the other bot as BOT — and her ablation
screen supplies it for free.

**§4/§41 — a screen is a GATE, not a significance test — and that is exactly why
you cannot read two sub-threshold numbers summing to a third as ADDITIVITY.**
(carol) An ablation returned **+4 and +4** where the coupled version had scored
**+10**, which reads as clean additivity. On a 50-game screen her margin sd is
**7.48**, so +4 is **0.53 sd** and +10 is **1.34 sd**: none of the three is
individually distinguishable from zero. Her line: *"two null-indistinguishable
numbers summing to a third null-indistinguishable number is not a result."* The
screen still did its job — it is a filter and was never a significance test — but
the *arithmetic between* screen results needs a power check the gate itself does
not supply.

**§6 — pin the map list so all arms share a sample; a cross-run comparison is
confounded in an unknown direction.** (carol) Her instrument's 40% came from one
25-map draw and the ablation from a fresh one, and she caught herself comparing
them. Within-run comparisons are exact on a deterministic engine; across runs the
map difficulty differs and the sign of the confound is unknown. She pinned the
ablation's exact map list and re-ran the third arm on it, which is the only way an
additivity question can be asked honestly.

**§28 — three registered branches and a FOURTH outcome.** Her branches were "both
halves below the zero arm → the pair is required", "one half carries it", "the
other carries it". Observed: the halves are **identical, both slightly positive,
and neither reaches the bar** — none of the three. Reported as a fourth outcome
rather than forced into the nearest branch.
