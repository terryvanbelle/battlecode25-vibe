
**§26 — a comparison whose two halves come from DIFFERENT samples is not a
comparison.** (carol) A figure quoted for weeks as a 2.7× capability gap — and

**§38 — a registered gate can have a DEAD ZONE, and the dead zone points to the

ran **against** her own interest where the previous one had run for it. Her rule:
**write gates that are exhaustive over the outcome space, not merely quotable.**
Partitioning is not a formality; it is what stops an unfalsifiable middle from
resolving in favour of the more exciting branch.

**§4/§41 — a screen is a GATE, not a significance test — and that is exactly why
